The Story of Ram Bhai

More than 25 years ago, Ram Bhai set up a small stall near the bus stand with nothing but a stove, a kettle, and a recipe passed down from his mother.

Today, that same stall is where the village meets. Farmers, students, teachers — everyone starts their day here. Same spot. Same taste.

No machines, no packets. Every cup is brewed fresh, with full-cream milk from the local dairy and spices ground by hand each morning.

Chai sirf peeni nahi hoti, mehsoos bhi karni hoti hai.

— Ansh Bhai, Stall Owner
